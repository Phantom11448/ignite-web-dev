02 — How It Works

From first call to live site.

Four stages, no mystery. Here's exactly what happens between hiring me and having a site that works.

01

Discovery

A free quote call — what you need, who it's for, what "done" looks like.

We start with a conversation, not a contract. I want to know who your customers are, what they need to be able to do on the site, and what problem the current setup is causing you. You leave that call with a real number and a real timeline — not a range designed to get you in the door.

02

Design

A real layout built around your brand, not a recycled template.

No theme marketplace, no drag-and-drop builder that looks like every other site in your industry. The layout gets built around your actual content and your actual brand. You see it before a single line of production code gets written, and changes at this stage cost nothing but time.

03

Build

Fast, clean, hand-coded — fully responsive, no bloat.

Everything is written by hand. That means no plugin stack to maintain, no bloated page weight, and no mystery code you can never get out from under. Responsive from the first breakpoint, tested on real devices, and built to score well on the performance metrics Google actually measures.

04

Launch

Deployed, tested, and handed off with support after go-live.

Deployment, DNS, SSL, search console, sitemap — handled. You get walked through how to make basic updates yourself, and I don't vanish the day it goes live. If something breaks in week three, you have someone to call.
